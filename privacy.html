Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

This website, zaanfarzaan.site (“the Website”), is run by Farzan Khan, who writes under the pen name Zaan Farzaan (“we”, “us”, “our”). This page explains, in plain words, what information the Website handles, why, and what you can ask us to do about it. The Website does not sell your information and does not show advertisements.

1. What you give us, and why

Comments. If you post a comment under a ghazal, nazm or audio, we store the name you type and your comment. Comments are reviewed before they appear; once approved, the name and comment are shown publicly on that page and in “Readers’ Voices” on the home page. Please don’t use your full real name if you’d rather not have it public.

“Share a feeling” ideas. If you send a feeling or idea for a future poem (the name is optional), it is stored and read only by the poet. It is not shown publicly.

Feedback form. The name, email address and message you type are sent to our inbox through the form service Formspree, so that we can read and, if needed, reply.

Newsletter. If you subscribe, your email address is handled by the newsletter service Buttondown, which sends the emails. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

eBook orders. Payment is made in your own UPI app, directly to our UPI ID. We never see your bank or card details. To deliver the eBook we only use what you choose to send us afterwards — the payment screenshot or UTR number, and your email address or Instagram handle.

Emails and messages. If you write to us by email or Instagram, we keep the conversation only as long as needed to reply and to keep a record of an eBook order.

2. Visit statistics

We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service, to count visits: which pages are opened, roughly where visitors come from (for example, a link or search engine), and general device details such as browser and screen size. It does not use cookies, does not build a profile of you, and does not show any pop-up. These statistics are visible only to the site owner.

3. What stays on your own device

To make the Website work the way you left it, your browser stores small pieces of information on your own device (“local storage”): your chosen language and light/dark theme, your favourite poems, which poems you have read and which audios you have played, the “where you left off” card, your game results, your visit-day streak and badges, and whether you closed the “install as an app” prompt. This information is not sent to us. You can remove it any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings. The installable app (PWA) also saves copies of pages on your device so poems can be read offline.

4. Other services the Website relies on

  • Supabase — stores comments and ideas.
  • Formspree — delivers feedback-form messages to our inbox.
  • Buttondown — newsletter sign-ups and sending.
  • GoatCounter — anonymous visit statistics.
  • Google Fonts — the Website’s fonts are loaded from Google, so your browser contacts Google’s servers (which can see your IP address) when a page opens.
  • cdnjs (Cloudflare) — delivers a script used to turn a sher into a downloadable image.
  • GitHub — hosts the Website’s files.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy and handles data under its own terms. Links to Instagram or other sites take you away from this Website, and their policies apply there.

5. Keeping and removing information

Comments, ideas and feedback are kept until they are no longer useful or until you ask us to remove them. To have a comment, idea, feedback message or email removed, write to zaanfarzaan1@gmail.com (mention the page and the name used, so we can find it). You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the link in any email.

6. Children

The Website is meant for a general audience and is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please write to us and we will remove it.

7. Changes to this policy

If the Website starts handling information in a new way, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.

8. Contact

For any question about this policy, use the Contact page or write to zaanfarzaan1@gmail.com.
